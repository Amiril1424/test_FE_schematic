Annotation policy update

Shared policy: schematic_annotation_policy.js.
Cartesian priority Q1 (right/up), Q2 (left/up), Q3 (left/down), Q4 (right/down).
SVG Y is reversed centrally in the quadrant definitions.

Diameter: both witness origins stay on the original engineering section.
The horizontal and vertical offsets are always equal, maintaining 45 degrees.
Each quadrant tries paper-mm projections 18,16,14,12,10,8 before the next quadrant.
These correspond to diagonal lengths about 25.5 to 11.3 mm.
Text, arrows and the dimension bar move together.
Witness lines may traverse the measured section itself; unrelated components
and annotation obstacles are checked. The label avoids all component geometry.

Leaders use the same ordered quadrants and descending nominal lengths.
Pole anchor selection switches to the matching pole face before mirroring.
The existing compact OHW route options (down to 4 mm horizontal projection)
remain available within each quadrant for multi-line labels between height levels.
Higher-priority quadrants win over a shorter line in a later quadrant.

Tests: v2-quadrants.cjs checks 45 degrees, length bounds, upper Q1 and default
Q1 leader. Existing final, content-parity, leader and 54-case dimension tests pass.

Shorter-leader/text update:
Default projection 18 -> 9 mm; diameter candidates 9,8,7,6,5,4 mm.
Default 45-degree diagonal 25.46 -> 12.73 mm. Congested leader fallback
maximum 32 -> 16 mm, considered only after normal candidates in all quadrants.
Leader letter spacing 0.12 mm and additional word spacing 0.35 mm; both are
included in measured label widths and exported SVG. Font remains 3 mm.

Lower diameter and vertical leader spacing update:
Upper diameter remains 9 mm projection. Lower diameter uses 12,11,10 mm
projections (default diagonal 16.97 mm). Visible lower witness strokes start
outside the measured pole envelope; original reference points remain in metadata.
Leader row spacing is 4.6 mm. First baseline is 1.5 mm above the leader line;
second baseline is 4 mm below it. Collision bounds use these same positions.
Final fit may enlarge an already-fitting drawing to open vertical gaps between
OHW levels; simply shrinking such a drawing would reduce the available gap.

Moderate text spacing and downward diameter correction:
Leader row spacing is now 4.1 mm (between the former 3.6 and 4.6 mm).
First baseline is 1.3 mm above the rule; second baseline 3.7 mm below.
For Q3/Q4 diameter witnesses, both reference points use the same 0.5 mm
projected start gap, preserving equal diagonal lengths and the measured diameter.
Q3 labels sit outside left; Q4 labels outside right. The dimension bar extends
under the side label. Upward diameter placement is unchanged.

Datum line sizing update:
GL half-length is at least 18 paper mm and grows with object radius, text
width and clearance. Both sides remain symmetric. Inspection text begins
8 paper mm beyond its reference target; underline length is at least 24 mm
and grows with measured text. Vertical dimension bundles move outward if
necessary rather than compressing the reference line; 1 mm gaps are retained.

Object-height column compaction:
Final placement uses schematic_height_layout.js after leaders are placed. Each
vertical span reserves its own object/leader envelope plus 5 paper mm; adjacent
columns retain at least 8 mm. Engineering origins and dimension values stay fixed.
Taper load-centroid labels now use the parent H index with an apostrophe, H2'=...
without consuming another object-height index.
