Adjacent pole specification rule:
For each upper taper / lower straight pair, matching joint diameter, wall thickness
and material suppress the repeated straight thickness/material rows. Keep its diameter.
Keep the taper specification on the taper. Different materials retain both specifications.
Load-center and opening dimensions share one lane where their vertical spans are disjoint,
so the opening follows the pole segment column at the normal paper spacing.

Diameter correction: matching straight sections must use a true diameter dimension
(two parallel 45-degree witnesses, opposite arrows, horizontal dimension line),
never a single-arrow diameter leader. Anchor at both actual pole surfaces at the
lower section endpoint. Equal witness gaps preserve the measured diameter. Only
pole diameter witness lines may cross pole object lines; text must remain clear.

LATEST matching-chain rule:
Evaluate every adjacent joint top to bottom. Matching joint diameters, thicknesses
and materials connect a chain. If that chain contains a taper, all straight sections
in it (above or below the taper) omit their specification leaders and retain true
upper/lower diameter dimensions. Coincident equal joint dimensions are drawn once
with both source IDs. A mismatch breaks the chain. Keep the taper specification.
Arm length rows move upward as complete annotations when crossing pole diameter
text, retaining their engineering extension origins.
