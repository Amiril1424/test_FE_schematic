STAGE 5–6 INITIAL IMPLEMENTATION
Module: schematic_leaders.js. Preview: obsolete/previews/leaders_preview.html.
Stage 5 samples visible SVG geometry in paper coordinates once per placement pass,
collects dimension/text obstacles, and caches text measurements across retry passes.
Stage 6 priorities: arm specifications, arm lighting, OHW, Direct Object, pole.
Arm specifications group matching height/diameter/thickness/material; alternative
arm anchors are available. OHW uses the real R250 cable arc and exact supplied text.
Pole anchors can move along their own segment and mirror left/right.
Candidates prefer above then below; line crossings with dimension lines allowed,
text boxes cannot overlap dimension obstacles. Bounded 0/4/8 mm lane expansion.
Failures are returned in issues and displayed in the preview.

Verification: three combined fixtures placed 6/7/6 leaders; the dense short-pair
fixture required an 8 mm dimension shift. Deliberately blocked fixture reports an
issue. Existing 9 dimension preview fixtures and 54-case stage-4 matrix still pass.

Limits for final review: curve obstacles are conservatively sampled at 2 paper mm;
search is bounded, not a guarantee for all inputs. Dimension-line vs arrow roles
remain shared in current renderer. Lane expansion is a provisional renderer-based
operation and requires broader final page-edge/annotation review. Opening/cross-
section leaders are not included because their V2 geometry/content is not built.
This is an isolated V2 preview, not a replacement of the main legacy application.
